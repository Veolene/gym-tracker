Weekly Progress

Progressive Overload

Aim to increase weight by 1-2.5 kg OR add 1-2 reps each week. Small consistent progress leads to big gains!

Rest Between Sets

Compound exercises: 2-3 minutes
Isolation exercises: 60-90 seconds

Rep Ranges

Strength: 4-6 reps
Hypertrophy: 8-12 reps
Endurance: 15-20 reps

Nutrition Basics

Protein: 1.6-2.2g per kg bodyweight
Water: 3-4 liters daily
Sleep: 7-9 hours

Form Tips

• Control the negative (lowering) phase
• Full range of motion
• Mind-muscle connection
• Don't ego lift - proper form first!

Signs to Deload

• Strength decreasing for 2+ weeks
• Feeling constantly fatigued
• Joint pain or discomfort
• Poor sleep quality
